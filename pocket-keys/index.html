MIDI messages

This is what a MIDI keyboard sends to a computer. It isn't sound, only instructions: which key, how hard, and whether it went down or up. The three codes at the end are the actual bytes (tiny numbers) that travel down the cable.

  • Play a note and its message appears here.

How to play

  • Mouse or finger: press a key. Slide across keys to glide. Press lower on a key to play louder (that's called velocity).
  • Computer keyboard: the row A S D F… plays white keys; W E T Y U… plays black keys. The letter is printed on each key.
  • Z / X move down / up an octave (8 white keys = the same notes, lower or higher). Space holds Sustain.
  • Try: C + E + G together (a happy chord), then A + C + E (a sad one).
